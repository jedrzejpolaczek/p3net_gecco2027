\paragraph{Representation and linkage tree.} A linkage tree is built from the current population, reflecting statistical dependencies between genotype variables: the per-edge operation choices of Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation}, extended below to include the discretised $\Theta$ coordinates once the joint setting is in scope. Following the standard P3 procedure, the tree is constructed by agglomerative, UPGMA style hierarchical clustering over a normalised mutual information based dependency measure between genotype variables, yielding the nested family of variable subsets used by the optimal mixing operator (Figure~\ref{fig:linkage-tree}); this construction is applied to the NAS genotype unchanged from its original combinatorial optimisation form. P3Net adopts option~(i) from Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation}: each continuous coordinate of $\Theta$ is discretised into a finite set of bins, fixed once before search (e.g.\ a log-spaced grid for learning rate), so that the joint genotype $\Lambda_1 \times \cdots \times \Lambda_n \times \Theta$ is entirely categorical and the linkage tree, its mutual-information-based dependency measure, and the optimal mixing sweep described below apply to it exactly as to the architecture-only encoding, with no separate real-valued mixing mechanism. This trades the resolution of the continuous hyperparameter search for keeping the central comparison against NSGA-II/NSGANetV2 attributable to the search engine and surrogate alone, rather than conflating it with an additional, separately novel continuous-mixing extension to P3; extending to option~(ii) (real valued mixing in the spirit of RV-GOMEA~\citep{andreadis2024maxclique}) is left as future work.

\paragraph{Crossover.} Crossover operates on the level of variable groups identified by this tree, rather than individual bits, protecting detected groups of related design decisions from being disrupted.

\paragraph{No mutation operator.} P3Net, like the wider P3/GOMEA lineage it draws on~\citep{goldman2014parameterless,thierens2011optimal}, has no separate mutation step: all variation is produced by the crossover-guided optimal mixing sweep described below, so population diversity comes entirely from recombination against the linkage tree rather than from an additional random perturbation operator.

\paragraph{Surrogate.} The surrogate's construction in P3Net exploits the same dependency structure that guides the variation operator: the relative, linkage aware estimator $\hat{\delta}_F$ defined in Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation}, trained on pairwise fitness differences derived from $\mathcal{H}_t$, is what P3's optimal mixing sweep queries at every proposed modification. Mechanistically this regression-based estimator is closer to CS-GOMEA's relative surrogate~\citep{dushatskiy2019csgomea} than to eLyMPuS's discrete comparison; the debt to eLyMPuS, a variant of LyMPuS~\citep{przewozniczek2026lympus}, is philosophical rather than mechanistic. Both predict relative fitness change conditioned on an incompletely discovered linkage structure rather than one assumed known in advance (Section~\ref{sec:related-work} elaborates), adapted here to network architecture encoding.

\paragraph{Telescoping construction.} An estimate of a candidate's absolute error is obtained compositionally, by telescoping this relative correction back along the sweep to the nearest ancestor with a known, fully evaluated value of $f_1$: writing $x_0, x_1, \dots, x_m = x'$ for the chain of tentatively accepted modifications since that ancestor $x_0$ (with $f_1(x_0)$ known), each step contributing $\hat{\delta}_{F_i}(x_{i-1}, x_i)$ for the linkage subset $F_i$ modified at step $i$,
\[
    \hat{f}_1(x_m) = f_1(x_0) - \sum_{i=1}^{m} \hat{\delta}_{F_i}(x_{i-1}, x_i),
\]
which reduces to $\hat{f}_1(x') = f_1(x) - \hat{\delta}_F(x, x')$ in the single step case $m=1$. The chain depth $m$ is bounded by $\kappa$, introduced below, precisely to limit how far this accumulated surrogate error can propagate before a full evaluation resets it.

\paragraph{Search loop.} Figure~\ref{fig:p3net-loop} summarises one iteration; the loop proceeds as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item P3 generates a candidate set $C \subset \Lambda^*$ through crossover guided by the linkage tree; for each parent selected for variation, every linkage subset in the current dependency model is visited in random order, following the standard optimal mixing sweep, and for each subset $F$ a donor is drawn from the population to propose a modification restricted to $F$ (Parent, donor, and ancestor provenance, below);
    \item every proposed modification $x'$ is scored by the surrogate relative to its immediate predecessor in the sweep, $\hat{f}_1(x')$, obtained via $\hat{\delta}_F$;
    \item a modification is tentatively accepted if $\hat{\delta}_F$ predicts nonnegative improvement (a zero acceptance threshold by default; Section~\ref{sec:results} assesses sensitivity to this threshold jointly with $\kappa$), and the sweep continues from the resulting individual; otherwise the modification is rejected, the individual is left unchanged, and the sweep proceeds to the next linkage subset in the order fixed in step~1;
    \item once every parent's sweep has run to completion, or has been curtailed by the chain depth limit $\kappa$ described below, a promising subset $C^* \subseteq C$ is selected among all sweep outcomes as the candidates nondominated in the $(\hat{f}_1, f_2)$ objective space ($C^*$ is an iteration-local selection set, distinct from the global front $\mathcal{P}^*$ of Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation} that the search approximates over time);
    \item each $x \in C^*$ undergoes a full evaluation, yielding the true value $f_1(x)$;
    \item $\mathcal{H}_t$ is updated, $\hat{\delta}_F$ is retrained, and the linkage tree is rebuilt as new data arrives, evolving alongside the population; within a single iteration, the same tree built at the start of that iteration is used for every parent's sweep in step~1, and is not rebuilt again until the next pass through step~6.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Parent, donor, and ancestor provenance.} Parents for step~1, and the ancestor $x_0$ used in the telescoping construction (above), are always drawn from individuals that have completed steps~5--6, i.e.\ elements of $\mathcal{H}_t$ with a genuinely known $f_1$, never from the transient, surrogate-only individuals produced mid-sweep; the telescoping construction therefore never bottoms out on a surrogate estimate. \emph{(TODO: the donor drawn within step~1 is not yet subject to this same stated restriction; clarify whether donors, like parents and the ancestor $x_0$, must also come from $\mathcal{H}_t$, or may also be transient, surrogate-only individuals from within the same sweep.)}

\paragraph{Fidelity scope.} P3Net operates exclusively at full fidelity $r_K$: the lower-fidelity apparatus introduced in Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation} is substrate-level machinery, instantiated where applicable in Section~\ref{sec:results}, but is not consumed by the search loop above. Training the surrogate on lower-fidelity observations as well is a design alternative discussed in Section~\ref{sec:conclusions}.

\paragraph{Constraint handling.} Candidates violating the constraint $g(x) \leq 0$ are rejected prior to surrogate scoring (i.e.\ before step~2 of the loop above).

\paragraph{Deduplication.} Duplicates already present in $\mathcal{H}_t$ skip reevaluation. This is applied identically to every compared method as part of the shared evaluation harness (Section~\ref{sec:results}, Fairness controls), so the saving is not specific to P3Net; it is a safeguard of substantial practical relevance given the genotype-to-phenotype redundancy documented in NSGA-Net (Section~\ref{sec:introduction}). The computational cost $f_2$ itself is computed analytically, without surrogate involvement (Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation}), so selection of candidates for full evaluation relies on nondomination in the $(\hat{f}_1, f_2)$ space.

\paragraph{Chain depth ($\kappa$).} To bound the accumulation of surrogate error along a sweep, a maximum chain depth $\kappa$ of tentatively accepted modifications based only on the surrogate is imposed: once $\kappa$ modifications have been accepted since the last full evaluation along a given sweep, the current individual is forced into $C^*$ regardless of its predicted $\hat{f}_1$. $\kappa$ is initialised at eLyMPuS's own $2\lceil\log_2(n)\rceil$-step bound, as a heuristic default, not as a derivation from the monotonicity guarantee that bound relies on, which does not apply here. Here $n$ is the dimensionality of the full extended, discretised genotype $\Lambda_1 \times \cdots \times \Lambda_n \times \Theta$, matching the scope of the linkage tree itself, not the architecture-only count of Section~\ref{sec:problem-formulation}; its sensitivity, jointly with that of the step-3 acceptance threshold (also a heuristic default, fixed at zero above), is assessed empirically rather than fixed by assumption (Section~\ref{sec:results}).

\paragraph{Design rationale (adapted from eLyMPuS).} Three further design choices follow from adapting the eLyMPuS mechanism to this setting:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Candidate generation follows the full optimal mixing sweep described in step~1, rather than a single modification per candidate, so that P3Net remains consistent with the variation operator on which P3's own performance guarantees rely.
    \item eLyMPuS's guarantee of recovering a missing dependency within $2\lceil\log_2(n)\rceil$ steps relies on a monotonicity assumption about the underlying fitness landscape that is not expected to hold for neural network accuracy; $\hat{\delta}_F$ is therefore treated as a learned heuristic estimator without that guarantee, and its reliability is instead assessed empirically through the surrogate quality analysis reported in Section~\ref{sec:results}.
    \item Nondomination in step~4 is restricted to candidates produced within the same search iteration, i.e.\ propagated from ancestors evaluated against the same snapshot of $\mathcal{H}_t$, so that compared $\hat{f}_1$ estimates share a common reference point rather than being pooled across iterations with a potentially drifting surrogate.
\end{itemize}
