\section*{Fixed-budget tables}

Median and interquartile range of the primary metric per arm, with the Holm--Bonferroni-corrected paired
Wilcoxon $p$ and Cliff's $\delta$ of each arm against P3Net, corrected within each (search space, budget) cell.
The primary metric is hypervolume relative to the frozen best-known front on the three JAHS-Bench-201 datasets
(higher is better) and normalised IGD+ against the exact oracle front on NAS-HPO-Bench-II (lower is better).
In both cases a positive $\delta$ favours P3Net.
